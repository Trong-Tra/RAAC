## Direction contract

THESIS: This surface is a printed lexicon plate of peer review — each paper is an entry, each council a plate of five examiner reports — and it refuses the dark developer-console dashboard this category always ships.

OWN-WORLD: Uncoated cream stock (#f4efe4), engraving-black ink (#1a1712), hairline rules and double-rule plate frames, small-caps letterspaced kickers, diamond ornaments. Vermilion (#b7341f) carries the live action and the "you are here" mark. Five mineral inks — ultramarine, emerald, madder rose, sepia, violet — carry the five reviewers' lenses; chrome yellow flags pending states. Scotch-modern serif display (Source Serif 4 as the Miller Text stand-in), mono reserved for registry codes and hashes. Paper-fiber quiet, engraved-line crisp.

STORY: A judge or author sees a paper typeset like a scholarly entry — submitted, ranked, signed, council, anchored — believes the process is rigorous, legible, and academic, and acts by submitting a paper and watching the council plate fill in report by report.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Guide line across the top (RAAC · Vol. 84532 · entry identifier · nav). Below, the paper's headword title as a large serif entry with an italic standfirst (fee escrowed, hash). Main column: Routing and Council sections set as lexicon entries, with a framed plate of the five examiner reports (name — lens, score · verdict, each in its mineral ink). Right column: the anchoring plate with transaction hashes. Far-left shoulder index: submitted / ranked / signed / council / anchor, the current step in vermilion with a thumb mark. Primary action sits as a vermilion plate-button directly under the entry.

FORM: Chosen form is the shoulder-and-plate lexicon page, a dealt challenger that won on both axes (audience identification, product clarity) over the assigned scientific poster session (position 5 of 7 on my grounded list).
